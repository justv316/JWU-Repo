My Review


Oklahoma City's Chat Pile delivers a tremendous performance on Who Loves the Sun, there 4th studio release. My personal inception to the band, I got hooked immediately, and found myself seeing them live not too long ago. Between each song Ray would talk to the crowd about his special interest, movies. It was charming, and worth noting in this review. He's a person, like me. Struggling and suffering through the horrors of capitalism, just like all of us.

This album is a cry for relief from the predatory system that we live in, late-stage capitalism is ruining us as a species, extracting every ounce of value from every aspect of life, including human life itself. We live in a society governed by individuals guilty of the most heinous crimes imaginable, and we can do nothing about it.


"Me and my dead children far away

And you will never hold them again"


Through the bleak atmosphere of this album, there is a glimmer of hope shining somewhere. This is mirrored in the reality Ray sings about, despite how awful things are, in spite of the capitalist ruling class who seeks to undermine the values of humanity in pursuit of a dollar, there is hope. There is community. There is your Sacred Green Room... Life is far too short to be spent enslaved by a system that runs on human blood and viscera.

Deprive them of effective labor.


Take back everything you can.
