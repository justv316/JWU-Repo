My Review


A refreshing break from the back-to-back traumabonding episodes that were the previously reviewed albums, the debut album of Man/Woman/Chainsaw, Cannonball gives us a sappy, artsy, folksy, fun time. While the relationship contexts in the songs are likely heteronormative, I can find connections to my real life in basically every word. Having recently gotten out of a very serious relationship, and still dealing with those lingering heartaches, these songs speak to me. Especially 'Only Girl', despite not being monoamorous myself, there is siginficant appeal in being 'owned', being 'someones.' And while my life is not centered around the pursuits of the heart, at this time, my heart longs for those feelings of being possessed.


Show me Slowly

I'm your only girl


MCW's unique blend of instrumentation, including 3 lead vocalists, multiple keyboardists and producers, leads to a beautiful experience unlike really any other album I've heard this album. It's punchy and emotional when it wants to be and whimsical when we need a break. Goddamn, Lizard Man! is an outright silly tune that makes me feel like I'm adjacent to a King Gizzard or Psychaedlic Porn Crumpets project, and that's just a compliment.
